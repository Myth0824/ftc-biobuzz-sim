1. Open Game Setup (⚙ in the control bar) and pick your Periods and Opponents. 2. Your robot is 32294, marked You (red alliance, right start). The turret (team 32294)

Your robot always has a full 360° turret. It starts aimed straight forward. Use ← → arrows (keyboard) or D-pad (controller) to turn it at 180°/s: right is clockwise. The arc shows where the next shot lands, and the third-person camera swings with the turret. The turret holds its angle when you release the keys. With the planner or an AUTO plan driving, the turret aims itself at the raised CELL, so it shoots from any heading.

Intake

Hold ↓ (keyboard) or LT (controller) to run the intake. Release to stop. The AI robots intake automatically. Picking up opponent NECTAR (blue balls) is allowed but triggers a G408 minor foul (+5 pts to the opponent).

Scoreboard

You marks your robot; the planner drives the others. A gear opens a robot's config. The gears hide during a MATCH: pause to get them back.

Status bar

The bar at the bottom of the screen shows the controller's name and whether it is connected, and which robot the keyboard drives. A blue dot means keys are held right now. It tucks itself above the AUTO editor's bar while you edit a plan.

Timeline and review

The scrubber bar shows the full match. Gray ticks = MATCH start, TELEOP start, FLOWERS open (1:00), endgame (0:20), and MATCH end. Colored dots = notes. Drag the thumb back during a live MATCH to review it while the MATCH waits. Press V or click the Review button to enter full review mode with free-look camera. Press Go Live or R to return.

AUTO path editor

Your robot runs an AI plan during the 30 s AUTO. To edit it: open Game Setup → Edit AUTO paths, or click the gear ⚙ on 32294, choose a plan, and click Edit AUTO paths. The ? button in the editor bar opens this help without leaving the editor. To bring in a Pedro Pathing auto, click Import Pedro Pathing auto in the robot config and pick the OpMode's .java file: its paths, intake, launches, and waits become a new plan. You can also drop the file on the page.
Orange dotsEach dot is a pose (position + heading). Drag to move it. Arrow on dotThe robot's heading at that position. Drag the tip to rotate. Click a dotOpens Step settings for that drive step: the intake, the turret, and a shoot. Step settingsTick Intake to run it during the drive, with a filter (POLLEN, own NECTAR, or all). Tick Turret to aim it to an angle while driving. Tick Shoot to fire a number of balls right after the step, with a timeout. Adding one to a bare drive step wraps the step in a parallel, and removing them unwraps it again. Steps panelThe list at the right while you edit: click a step to select it, ↑ and ↓ to reorder it, and ✕ to remove it. A shoot that a step added travels with it. Reference pointsNamed positions that steps can follow. Right-click one for Go to reference point, which adds a drive step to it. Add one with Add reference point here, and delete it from the same menu. Create newMakes a new plan: a copy of any existing one, or blank (Start from scratch) for the robot's start position. Edit buttonUnlocks dragging for your plan (system plans are read-only) Undo / RedoCmd+Z / Shift+Cmd+Z Reset this stepPuts a step back to its original position Delete planRemoves your custom plan SnapSnaps drags to a 1-inch grid and turns to 5°. Hold Alt, or Option on a Mac, to do the opposite.

The editor covers most plans. For waypoints, waits, conditions, and the camera's sightings, see the JSON reference below.

AUTO path JSON reference

A path is a JSON file with four sections: defs (named positions), root (the steps), and a few header fields. All positions use red-alliance coordinates: x = red wall (−) to blue wall (+), y = audience (−) to rear (+), headingDeg = degrees CCW from +x axis.
pose(x, y, deg)Define a field position, e.g. pose(−0.5, 1.2, 90) offset(p, dx, dy)Move a named pose by dx, dy meters field.hiveXX of the HIVE pivot (−0.32 m) field.halfHalf the field (1.79 m) field.flowerFar / NearFlower X and Y distances from center bots.me.dimensions.lengthRobot length, for offsets from a wall clock.remainingSeconds left, for waitUntil conditions

Actions (ref):

drive.driveToDrive to a pose. Params: pose, timeoutSec drive.followPathFollow waypoints in order. Params: waypoints, timeoutSec drive.pushPush straight. Params: power (−1 to 1), timeoutSec shooter.shootFire balls. Params: count, timeoutSec, cell (optional) intake.runRun the intake while the steps beside it in a parallel run. Params: filter (all, pollen, own_nectar, or none) turret.angleAim turret to degrees. Params: degrees, timeoutSec. 0=forward, 90=left, −90=right, 180=behind turret.holdHold turret at current angle. No params. waitWait fixed time. Params: timeoutSec waitUntilWait for condition. Params: condition (expression), timeoutSec vision.waitTipWait for HIVE to tip. Params: cell (rear/audience), timeoutSec

Structure keywords: sequence runs children in order. parallel policy:any runs all children at once and stops when any one finishes: run the intake beside a drive step this way.
